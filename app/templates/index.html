About BITSGuessr

BITSGuessr is a GeoGuessr-inspired game built around the BITS Goa campus.

Developed over the course of a week in a frenzy bordering mania, the game cost sleepless days, uncaffeinated nights, and isolational starvation thats kickstarted my becoming into a Buddha.

The result you see is in the form of rounds that show you a real photo taken somewhere on campus, and a silly little map that changes more phases than the moon while you try to pinpoint the location.
The farther your guess, the less points you earn, based on completely arbitrary rules made under mental states that should disqualify one from walking down the hallway.

Press I key at any time to view the info that may or may not be helpful or visible.

The current version includes a couple dozen locations, with more on the way. And you, yes YOU can help by submitting your own photos!


I plan to add more challenges, new locations, leaderboards, multiplayer, and additional game modes in the unseeable future... or the unseeable present, idk my visions been blurry.

If you spot a bug, it was Odysseus' fault. But if you have ideas for improvements, or if you have a fun challenge that would put wordle #1644 to shame, feel free to get in touch. Contributions and suggestions are always*T&C Apply welcome.
